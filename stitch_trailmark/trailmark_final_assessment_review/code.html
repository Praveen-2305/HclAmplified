Which regularization technique is most effective for feature selection by driving some weights exactly to zero?

radio_button_checked L2 Regularization (Ridge) Your Answer
check_circle L1 Regularization (Lasso) Correct Answer

Trail Guide Insight

L2 shrinks weights evenly but rarely to exactly zero, meaning all features are kept. L1 (Lasso) acts as a feature selector by aggressively zeroing out less important features. Think of L1 as an editor crossing out entire sentences, while L2 is just turning down the volume of the whole paragraph.
